- **Formal clearance** on **"Thresan"** and **"Skyflag"** + the logo, in
  **Canada + US**, for *confusingly-similar* marks (not just exact), in:
  **Class 28** (games), **Class 9** (downloadable/software games),
  **Class 41** (entertainment), **Class 16** (printed matter — the comic).
  Go / no-go before more brand equity accrues.

  (intent-to-use ok?) ± Madrid Protocol. Which classes are worth the cost?

### 2. IP assignment to Limnology Research Corporation
- **Document the assignment** to the corp of: the **trademarks**, **copyright**
  (game art, rulebook, the Volume Zero comic, site/code), the **codebase/app**,
  and the **domains**. Clean, on-paper chain of title with the corp as owner.
- Should the **trademark applications be filed in the corp's name** from the
  outset (vs. assigning later)?

### 3. Copyright
- The comic (Volume Zero), rulebook, art, and site are auto-protected on
  creation — worth **registering** the key ones (esp. US, for statutory
  damages)?
- Note: game *mechanics* aren't protectable — the moat is brand + art + text
  + the free playable product.

### 4. Chain of title / AI-generated assets

  there may be freelance work. AI-image copyright is currently murky — what
  can the corp **claim**, and what should be **re-commissioned as clean
  work-for-hire** before print/manufacture?
